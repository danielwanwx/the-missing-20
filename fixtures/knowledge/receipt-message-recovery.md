title: Receipt message recovery procedure
allowed_use: PROCEDURE_ONLY
---

Before restarting a receipt message, compare the physical receipt, ERP receipt,
material-document history, invoice hold, and failed-message record for the same purchase
order line. Restart is a recommendation for the Integration Operator only; it never
grants permission or performs a mutation.
